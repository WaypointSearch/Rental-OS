Services

Five ways to build your team.

Choose the engagement that fits the role, the timeline and the risk — or combine them as your projects evolve.

01

Executive Search

A retained, research-driven search for the leaders who shape your firm — confidential, thorough and benchmarked against the full market.

  • Market map of every viable candidate
  • Structured leadership assessment
  • Weekly progress reporting

Best forPrincipals, partners, department heads, office and regional leaders.

02

Direct Hire

Contingent permanent placement for engineers, designers, project managers and field leaders — you pay only when you hire.

  • Technical screening by discipline
  • Licensure and reference verification
  • Replacement guarantee

Best forEIT through senior PE, PMs, superintendents and estimators.

03

Contract & Contract-to-Hire

Flexible technical talent on our payroll — absorb workload peaks, cover leave or try before you commit.

  • W-2 contractors, insured and compliant
  • Convert to permanent on clear terms
  • Payrolling for candidates you've found

Best forProject peaks, commissioning, BIM, field and design support.

04

Project Team Builds

Stand up a complete design or construction team for a new office, a new market or a major pursuit — recruited in parallel, on one timeline.

  • Org design and hiring plan
  • Dedicated delivery team and single point of contact
  • Volume pricing across multiple hires

Best forOffice openings, market expansion, large mission-critical and healthcare programs.

05

Market Intelligence

Compensation benchmarks, talent maps and competitor insight so your offers land and your hiring plan is grounded in reality.

  • Role-level salary and bonus benchmarking
  • Talent availability by market and license
  • Annual MEP & AEC salary guide

Best forComp reviews, new-market planning and hard-to-fill roles.
