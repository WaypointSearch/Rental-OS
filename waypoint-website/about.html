Our story

Built by people who know the industry.

Waypoint Search was founded on a simple belief: the engineers, architects and builders behind every hospital, data center and campus deserve recruiters who actually understand their work.

Too many firms were being sent keyword-matched resumes by generalist agencies. Too many great engineers were being pitched roles that didn't fit their license, their project experience or their goals.

So we built a different kind of firm — organized by discipline, grounded in technical knowledge, and measured by how long our placements stay and grow, not how many resumes we send.

Our approach

Specialization, relationships and rigor.

Three principles shape every search we run — whether it's a single designer or a new regional office.

Specialization

Our recruiters each own a discipline. They know the codes, the software, the credentials and the firms — so they can tell a strong candidate from a strong resume.

Relationships

We play the long game. Many of the engineers we place today will be the hiring managers we partner with tomorrow — so we treat both sides with the same care.

Rigor

Defined search plans, structured screening, verified licensure and transparent reporting. No resume spam, no surprises.

What we value

The standards we hold ourselves to.

Integrity

We tell clients and candidates the truth — about the market, the role and the fit.

Expertise

We invest in understanding the technical work so we can represent it accurately.

Accountability

We set clear timelines, report progress weekly and stand behind every placement.

Partnership

We measure success by the careers we build and the teams that stay together.
