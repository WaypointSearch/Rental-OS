Candidate FAQ

Questions engineers ask us.

Straight answers about how we work with candidates. Don't see yours? Ask us directly.

Does it cost anything to work with Waypoint?

No. Our services are always free for candidates. Employers pay our fees when they hire.

Will my current employer find out I'm looking?

Not from us. Your search is confidential, and we never send your resume to an employer without your explicit approval for that specific role.

Why don't you post all of your open jobs?

Most of our clients ask us to recruit confidentially — they may be replacing someone, entering a new market or building a team before announcing it. The role profiles on this page show the kinds of positions we fill; we'll share live searches with you directly when there's a fit.

What happens after I submit my resume?

A recruiter who focuses on your discipline reviews your background. If we're working on a search that fits, or expect to soon, we'll reach out to learn what you want next. Before any introduction, we'll walk you through the firm, the role and the compensation.

Do you only work with licensed engineers?

No. We recruit at every level — EITs, designers, technicians, project engineers and field staff through licensed PEs, RAs, principals and executives.

Can you help with contract or remote work?

Yes. We place contract and contract-to-hire professionals, and many design roles are hybrid or remote. Tell us your preferences when you submit your resume.
